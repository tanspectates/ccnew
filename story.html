THE BEFORE & AFTER.

Before: “I have nothing to wear.” After: “Actually, the shoes are doing most of the work.”

THE PROCESS?
ABSOLUTELY UNHINGED.

First comes the late-night group chat. Then a hundred saved references, three brutally honest opinions, and a silhouette that refuses to leave our heads.

We obsess over the familiar things: chunky soles, impossible angles, a shape that makes a plain tee look like a decision.

FOR THE ONES
WHO NOTICE.

Closet Cleaned is a concept for people who notice the shoes before the outfit, and know that both can be true at the same time.

This entire store is an experiment in how a sneaker brand should feel: a little dramatic, a little self-aware, and deeply committed to the fit.
